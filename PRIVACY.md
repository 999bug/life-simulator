# Privacy Policy

This is a single-player text life simulator (人生模拟器). It is designed to be privacy-friendly: there are no accounts, no ads, and no third-party analytics or tracking cookies.

## What we do not collect

- No email address, phone number, or real name is required or collected.
- No cookies, advertising identifiers, or third-party tracking.
- No in-game analytics are sent to us. The statistics and "data panel" are generated and stored only in your browser.

## Data stored locally in your browser

The game uses browser `localStorage` to persist your progress and settings, including save slots, achievements, statistics, family tree, theme, challenge history, and a randomly generated anonymous device ID.

This data stays on your device and is not sent to the developer. You can remove it at any time by clearing site data for this site.

## Optional leaderboard

If you play a Daily / Weekly / Seed challenge and the backend is enabled, your final result may be submitted to a public leaderboard:

- A self-chosen nickname (shown publicly on the leaderboard).
- Your final score, age, ending, and a compact summary (gender, attributes, cause of death, and life snapshots).
- A random anonymous device ID, used only for deduplication and rate limiting. It is not shown on the public leaderboard.

Your IP address is processed only transiently to enforce rate limits; it is not stored or logged.

## Hosting providers

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. The optional backend runs on Cloudflare Workers with Cloudflare KV. Leaderboard data is stored by Cloudflare and may be processed in the regions where Cloudflare operates. Please review GitHub's and Cloudflare's privacy policies.

## Data retention

Leaderboards keep the top 100 entries per board. Rate-limit counters expire automatically within a few minutes. We do not maintain server access logs.

## Children

This is a general-audience text life simulator. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this file.

## Contact

For privacy questions, please open a GitHub issue in the repository or contact the project owner.
